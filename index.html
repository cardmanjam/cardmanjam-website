Condition Guide

Photos are part of every product description. Raw cards are not guaranteed to receive any particular grade from PSA, CGC, Beckett, or another grading company.

Near Mint

May show minor manufacturing or handling imperfections while remaining generally clean.

Lightly Played

May show light edge wear, small scratches, minor whitening, or other visible handling wear.

Moderately Played

Shows more noticeable wear such as whitening, scratches, edge wear, or small creases.

Heavily Played or Damaged

May show major creases, dents, peeling, staining, writing, water damage, or other substantial defects.
